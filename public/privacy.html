Only what you type in the invite form: your email, where you are coming from, and, if you give them, your Mac's chip and memory and what you hope Ressac does for you. To pick about 20 beta testers and email them. Nothing else: no newsletter, no advertising, no resale.

In a Cloudflare D1 database restricted to the European Union. To keep bots out, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which sets no cookie.

How long

Until 6 months after the end of the beta, and never more than 12 months after you signed up. Then it is deleted.
